# The AI Transformation Operator's Playbook

*Frameworks, decision tools, and worked examples for selecting and sequencing AI workflows inside large enterprises.*

By Paul Kolesnik. A work in progress, published in public.

---

## Why this exists

Most AI transformation writing falls into one of two buckets: vendor pitch decks that treat AI as a tool to install, or research essays that treat AI as a phenomenon to admire. Neither is what an operator inside a 1,000-to-20,000-person company actually needs when they're handed a list of 200 candidate workflows and told to make it a roadmap.

This repo collects the frameworks I use, the decisions I make, and the worked examples I've shipped. It's the playbook I wish existed three years ago.
